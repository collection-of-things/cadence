# Cadence brand concept 01

Status: first concept for review. The app has not been changed.

## Idea

A quiet listening room for your music. The mark uses three open, concentric grooves to form a C. Repetition gives it rhythm; the open side keeps it light. The symbol should work without the wordmark in a desktop dock or sidebar.

## Palette

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm black | #0C0C0E | Main background |
| Graphite | #19181B | Raised surfaces |
| Warm white | #F5F2ED | Primary text and reversed mark |
| Apricot | #F4A377 | Main logo and selective accents |

These colors follow the existing v2 app brief. Album artwork can supply atmosphere while the identity stays consistent.

## Typography

Explore a lowercase Manrope wordmark with carefully adjusted spacing. This is a proposed brand type direction, not an instruction to replace the app font. The current app brief uses system fonts for reliable desktop rendering.

## Applications

- Primary lockup: apricot symbol and warm-white wordmark on warm black.
- Single-color versions: warm white on dark, or warm black on light.
- App icon: apricot symbol on a charcoal rounded square.
- Supporting pattern: enlarged groove arcs, used sparingly.
- Image direction: tactile record grooves, warm side light, quiet dark surfaces.

The board is a raster concept. Lettering and mark geometry need final vector construction after direction approval. Check the simplified mark at 16, 24, 32, and 64 pixels during that step; reduce detail if the grooves close up.

## Copy

Optional campaign line: “A place for your music.” Keep normal product screens concise.

## Next decision

Review the groove-C idea, its silhouette, and the warmth of the palette before producing final SVG logos and export sizes. No trademark clearance is implied by this concept.
