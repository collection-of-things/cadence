# Cadence brand concept 02

Revision: bring a music disc into the identity as a reusable asset.

The logo explores a solid C cut from a record silhouette, with a spindle-hole center. The supporting vinyl asset uses fine black grooves and an apricot center label. Repeat the disc in sleeve artwork, listening screens, and larger brand compositions.

Palette stays aligned with the app brief: #0C0C0E, #19181B, #F5F2ED, #F4A377.

This is a concept for review. The board explores serif display lettering alongside a sans wordmark; the final typography pairing and exact logo geometry remain undecided. The flat mark needs small-size review and vector construction before production use.

Files: concept-02.png is the identity board. music-disc-02.png is the separate transparent disc asset. The app has not been edited.
